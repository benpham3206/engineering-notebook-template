# Handoff: short title

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## State

Where the work stands, in two or three sentences. Link GOAL.md, STATUS.md, and the latest entry.

## Done

- What is finished and verified, with links to the evidence.

## Open

- What is started but not finished, and anything not verified.

## Next step

The one next action, with the files it touches and how to tell it is done.

## Hazards

- What could break, injure, or waste money if done wrong, and anything the next person must not change.
