# G001: Drill the wall

**Date:** 2025-10-27
**Status:** passed

## Step

Drill eight holes in the plaster wall: two for each enclosure and two for each spring idler.

## Cost if wrong

A hole in a hidden cable or pipe is a shock, fire, or flood hazard. A hole in the wrong place leaves a permanent mark that has to be filled and painted.

## Checklist

- [x] Scanned each hole position with a stud and cable detector: no cable or pipe found within 50 mm.
- [x] Held each printed enclosure on the rod with the curtain hung: the panel clears the enclosure through full travel.
- [x] Marked holes through a printed drilling template, not by measuring from the rod by eye.
- [x] Drill bit size matches the wall plugs in the [BOM](../../BOM.md), ref 11.
- [x] Both enclosures passed ten bench cycles in [the 2025-10-27 entry](../entries/2025-10-27-first-build-and-power.md), so the mount is unlikely to change.

## Decision

Go. Every check passed, and the template marks matched the rod brackets within 1 mm.
